# Screenshots

Every page of the dashboard and every plugin, taken from a running Quorvane 2.0.0 with demo data. The pictures show the whole page from top to bottom.

The dashboard follows the light or dark setting of your device; these pictures use the light look. Texts are shown in English here and exist in 12 languages.
